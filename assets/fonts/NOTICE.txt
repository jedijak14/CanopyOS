Canopy's script fallback fonts are Noto Sans CJK SC, TC, JP and KR, subset to
CJK, kana and Hangul ranges, from the Noto CJK project:
https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk (Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe and Google).
The Arabic, Devanagari, Hebrew and Thai fonts are Noto Sans regular, from the
@fontsource/noto-sans-{arabic,devanagari,hebrew,thai} 5.3.0 packages.
All these fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see
../hello/OFL.txt. The Canopy project does not claim ownership of the fonts.
The emoji fallback is Noto Emoji from @fontsource/noto-emoji 5.3.2, also under
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Copyright The Noto Project Authors).
